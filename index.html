About me

Cinema meets fashion.

Every look tells a story, and every frame is a chance to shine.

I'm Nazarian Lopez, a fashion model, actor and cinematographer working out of New York City. I was born in June 22nd 2012, and I collaborated with Trust for Public Land, my previous school M.S. 343, and Kidz Spotlight Magazine.

CineFashionX is the name I create under. It covers everything I do: acting on set, modeling for shoots and campaigns, walking the runway, and filming short films and clips from behind the camera.

I'm also proud to show up for the city at community events. If you've got a project, a brand or an idea, DM me or send an email. I'm always open to collabs.
